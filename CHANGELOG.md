# Changelog

## 0.24.0 (2026-10-19)

- Loot Master, in test mode: a looter window, raider popups, response buttons, Raider Score and an award message (`/crt loot test`).
- Loot History: the Response filter lists every response, and RCLootCouncil's awards are read in full with their dates.
- Timeline: a boss's planned timeline is its ability timeline everywhere; the Encounter Journal button is Export Abilities.
- Menus and submenus always show above their window.

## 0.23.0 (2026-10-18)

- Raid Notes: Shared and Personal tabs; guild notes labelled by guild, pickup-group notes kept apart; each note says who changed and shared it.
- Notes list: grouped, a dropdown or chips, and it scrolls.
- Encounter Journal: Export Abilities writes a boss's abilities to the Timeline's Advanced tab.
- Timeline: Journal icons on rows, phases named by stage, time labels that follow the zoom, Recent pulls in boxes.
- Personal note: a pasted raid note shows by phase, light markdown, Display my notes first.
- Alerts: speak when shown or at 0, a sound preview, and a sound when a debuff lands on you.
- Templates: Healthstone and Health potion first, Gate added.
- Loot History: boss loot recorded, matched to RCLootCouncil, listed and exported.
