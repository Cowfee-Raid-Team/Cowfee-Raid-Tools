# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-20)

- Themes: pick the look from the title bar: Default: Blizzard, Blackout: Midnight, Blackout: Nitro or Beanworks: Cowfee. Minimal black is gone.
- Modern look: new sliders (drag, click the knob to type a value, mouse wheel), walnut checkboxes, help icons, and window title bars that match the main window.
- Loot Master: items you can't use are always answered Transmog, weapons your class can't use are caught, any answer can be given the item, and every answer gets a roll.
- Loot Master: "X/Y answered" turns orange when someone timed out, the boss name shows in the title, All Loot Rolls lists every answer, and item tooltips show on the icon.
- Loot Master: Sync Master Loot sends your settings to an officer you pick and to your loot authority list, and a Loot filter tab sets which items open a session.
- Loot Master: the raid leader is asked "Use CRT Looting in this instance?" and can turn it on or off for the raid. It shows the state only; rolls aren't passed yet.
- Raider Score follows the Late Tracker's on-time and no-show rules.
- Debuffs: Preview debuffs, dispel icon size, borders and glow in the dispel type's color, and spacing that fits the border.
- Raid Notes: Show outside of raid.
- Timeline: Mythic ability timelines for Nymrissa Wavecaller and Kith'ix.
- Files are grouped into CRT_Core, CRT_Modules, CRT_Data, CRT_Media and CRT_Dev. Put your own sounds in the CowfeeRaidTools_Media addon or CRT_Media\Sounds; the old Sounds folder is no longer read.

## 0.24.0 (2026-10-19)

- Loot Master, in test mode: a looter window, raider popups, response buttons, Raider Score and an award message (`/crt loot test`).
- Loot History: the Response filter lists every response, and RCLootCouncil's awards are read in full with their dates.
- Timeline: a boss's planned timeline is its ability timeline everywhere; the Encounter Journal button is Export Abilities.
- Menus and submenus always show above their window.

## 0.23.0 (2026-10-18)

- Raid Notes: Shared and Personal tabs; guild notes labelled by guild, pickup-group notes kept apart; each note says who changed and shared it.
- Notes list: grouped, a dropdown or chips, and it scrolls.
- Encounter Journal: Export Abilities writes a boss's abilities to the Timeline's Advanced tab.
- Timeline: Journal icons on rows, phases named by stage, time labels that follow the zoom, Recent pulls in boxes.
- Personal note: a pasted raid note shows by phase, light markdown, Display my notes first.
- Alerts: speak when shown or at 0, a sound preview, and a sound when a debuff lands on you.
- Templates: Healthstone and Health potion first, Gate added.
- Loot History: boss loot recorded, matched to RCLootCouncil, listed and exported.
